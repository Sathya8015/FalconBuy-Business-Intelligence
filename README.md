## 📌 Project Overview

**FalconBuy** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence and AI Decision Support System developed for an omnichannel retail business.

The project transforms large-scale retail data into meaningful business insights using **MySQL, SQL, Python, Excel, and Microsoft Power BI**.

The solution analyzes multiple business functions including:

- Sales
- Customers
- Products
- Inventory
- Shipments
- Marketing
- Stores
- Executive performance

The project follows a complete analytics workflow from **data generation and database development to SQL analysis, Python EDA, Power BI modeling, dashboard development, and business decision support**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of FalconBuy are to:

- Analyze overall business and sales performance.
- Identify revenue and order trends.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Monitor inventory and stock availability.
- Analyze shipment and delivery performance.
- Evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness.
- Compare store and regional performance.
- Build centralized business reporting.
- Provide actionable business insights.
- Support data-driven management decisions.
- Establish a foundation for future AI-powered analytics.
